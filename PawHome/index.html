How Adoption Works at Paw Home

We guide you at every step so both you and your new pet feel confident from day one.

01

Browse & Inquire

Explore profiles above, save your favorites, and submit a quick 2-minute adoption or visit request online.

02

Meet & Greet

Spend unhurried time with your matched pet in our quiet playrooms with a dedicated adoption counselor.

03

Home Readiness

We review your living space, answer nutrition or training questions, and finalize vaccinations & microchipping.

04

Welcome Home

Take your companion home with medical records, starter food, a 30-day check-in, and lifetime rescue support.
