Our Story

Paw Home began in Islamabad as a student-led community initiative after we noticed that dozens of healthy rescued animals stayed in crowded shelters simply because local families did not know where to look or what health checks had been done.

Working alongside rescue centres in Sector F-7, Chaklala, and Gulberg, we built a single directory where shelters share verified vaccination records, temperament notes, and honest care needs before an adopter even schedules a visit.

Today, Paw Home guides both first-time and experienced pet parents through a clear five-step adoption process, transparent first-year budgeting, and local vet advice so every adoption turns into a lifelong match.

Our Core Values

Every listing and shelter partnership on Paw Home follows three guiding principles.

01

Compassion

We treat every rescued dog, cat, rabbit, and bird with patience and dignity, matching them with homes suited to their personality rather than rushing placement.

02

Safety

Every pet profile shows real vaccination status and shelter screening, and home visits ensure balconies, gates, and indoor spaces are safe before handover.

03

Responsibility

We help adopters plan realistic monthly food and vet budgets upfront and offer follow-up care guides so families feel prepared long after adoption day.
