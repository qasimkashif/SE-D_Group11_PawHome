Our Story

A local rescue network built in Islamabad

Paw Home started in Islamabad when a small group of students, volunteers, and veterinary students noticed how difficult it was to find trustworthy adoption listings. Many rescued dogs, cats, rabbits, and birds were shared only in scattered social media groups without clear health histories.

We partnered with shelters across Islamabad, Rawalpindi, and Lahore to bring every adoptable animal into one organized space. Each profile highlights vaccination records, temperament notes, and shelter details so families know what to expect before visiting.

Today, our community coordinates meet-and-greets, guides first-time pet owners, and works closely with volunteer helpers so every adoption opens space for another rescue in need.

Our Core Values

Every adoption decision at Paw Home is guided by three simple commitments.

Value 01

Compassion

We treat every rescued animal with patience and kindness, giving shy or recovering pets the time they need to feel secure before placement.

Value 02

Safety

We verify partner shelters, share honest vaccination records, and check household readiness so pets settle into safe, indoor-friendly spaces.

Value 03

Responsibility

Adopting is a multi-year promise. We educate families on nutrition, Islamabad vet care, and long-term commitment before pickup day.
